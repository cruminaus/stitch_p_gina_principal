About the Activity

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the heart of the Atacama Desert. Witness the otherworldly landscapes of the Moon Valley, with its surreal salt formations and towering sand dunes. As the sun sets, marvel at the vibrant colors painting the sky over Death Valley, creating a truly magical experience. This tour offers a perfect blend of adventure, natural beauty, and geological wonder.

Important Information

  • Please wear comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, and a hat.
  • Bring your own packed lunch and sufficient water.
  • Help us protect this unique environment: do not leave any trash on the trail.
  • Wildlife encounters are possible but not guaranteed. Please do not feed the animals.
  • This tour involves moderate walking and is not recommended for individuals with mobility issues.
